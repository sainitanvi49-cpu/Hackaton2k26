# EcoAI — Presentation Outline

1. Title: EcoAI — Air Quality Intelligence and Environmental Sustainability
2. Problem: fragmented air-quality information and limited citizen participation
3. Solution: monitor, forecast, explain, report, and coordinate action
4. Technology: React, TypeScript, FastAPI, SQLite, Open-Meteo, Recharts
5. Live forecast dashboard: pollutant concentrations, timestamps, source labels
6. Analytics: hourly PM2.5 / PM10 and tomorrow's model forecast
7. Explainability: provisional severity score with transparent limitations
8. Citizen reporting: issue submission and review lifecycle
9. EcoAI Assistant: contextual rule-based explanations
10. Green Priority Index: illustrative geospatial decision-support concept
11. Admin workflows: alerts, reports, interventions, CSV exports
12. Limitations and future work: official CPCB methodology, validated station history, ML model training, ESA raster integration, authentication
